## 操作顺序 / Operation Sequence

1. 打开桌面“文明航程.app”。先点击界面或按键，让浏览器启动音频；展开“配乐开”，确认有声音并调低音量。
   Open the desktop app. Click or press a key to unlock browser audio. Open the music controls and set a comfortable volume.
2. 使用演示专用航程，选择农业时代、内海（中国）。说明先讲鲧、再讲禹，最后到战国；传说不标一个虚构的公元年份。
   Use a demonstration voyage. Choose agriculture and the inner sea, China. Explain the order: Gun, Yu and then the Warring States period. Legends have no invented calendar year.
3. 沿罗盘驶向第一座岛。点“图片背景 · 鲧”，展示现代分水设施照片和来源说明；按Escape回到船上。
   Follow the compass to the first island. Open Gun's optional image notes, show the modern irrigation photograph and source credit, then press Escape to return.
4. 按E，听人物与玩家的字幕对话。空格推进。读清水面任务后，用WASD开进所选浮标门。说明这是眼前供给与两岸合作的取舍，并非一道知识问答。
   Press E, advance the dialogue with Space, read the task and steer through a buoy gate with WASD. Explain the immediate cost and the longer-term relationship.
5. 展示结算与航海日志。若继续下一站，前站留下的合作可省回3份补给；不为了赶时间声称已经完成未展示的章节。
   Show the result and log. Cooperation can return three supplies at the next scene. Do not claim completion of scenes that have not been shown.
6. 按B打开船坞。先免费预览基础船和第三级外观，再回到已拥有等级。补给充足时购买一次实际整修；人心达到门槛时购买行装。按钮禁用时展示所需资源，不临时修改余额。
   Press B. Compare the original ship with a free tier-three preview, then return to owned tiers. Buy an upgrade only if eligible; show the requirements when a button is disabled.
7. 返回海面，展示实际船型与原船员的行装。打开音乐控制，演示静音和恢复。不要把自由预览当作已购买的升级。
   Return to sea to show the real ship and crew. Demonstrate mute and resume. A free preview does not unlock an upgrade.
8. 用PPT展示另外七条路线和终点人物。总结由E接手：工具帮助整理与实现，试点要验证玩家是否理解技术选择的代价。RAG、付费线路和营收不是本版已完成成果。
   Use the deck to introduce the other seven routes and final speakers. Speaker E closes with tools and pilot validation. Retrieval, paid routes and revenue are not delivered claims.

## 出现问题时 / Recovery

- 小游戏按Escape可返回；最后一站退出后点“继续故事”再进入。
  Press Escape to leave a task. Use Continue Story to re-enter the final scene.
- 听不到声音时，先检查配乐和音效开关、音量，以及系统静音，再点开启重试。
  Check music, effects, volume and system mute, then retry playback.
- 展示使用演示专用存档。不要删除用户已有航程来制造“干净开场”。
  Use a dedicated demo save. Keep existing player voyages.
